# Retention summary

Draft for owner review — 20 October 2026. Retention periods, implementation evidence and approval remain to be confirmed.

## Scope

This section addresses data retention within the compliance review. The review also covers access logging and the third-party processor list; each area requires sign-off from a different owner.

The retention inventory must identify the data categories, systems and copies covered by the schedule. The categories below are proposed inventory entries for confirmation, rather than an assertion that each is present or in scope.

For each confirmed entry, the owner should provide the governing policy or other approved basis, the event that starts the retention period, the configured duration and evidence of disposal. Any exceptions or holds need an identified authority, review date and release process. These are proposed review requirements; implementation has not been verified.

## Access and approvals

The retention owner and approver have not yet been identified in the available materials. The final summary needs their name or role, approval date and a reference to the approved schedule. Approval of the access-logging section or processor list does not establish retention sign-off, because the review assigns these areas to different owners.

Access to retained data, permission to change retention settings and authority to approve exceptions remain to be documented and verified. Supporting evidence should identify the responsible roles and the relevant access and change records.

Before sign-off:

1. Confirm the retention owner, approver and inventory boundaries.
2. Complete the schedule with approved periods, start events and policy references.
3. Check system settings and disposal evidence, including relevant copies and processors.
4. Record exceptions, unresolved gaps and the owner's approval decision.
