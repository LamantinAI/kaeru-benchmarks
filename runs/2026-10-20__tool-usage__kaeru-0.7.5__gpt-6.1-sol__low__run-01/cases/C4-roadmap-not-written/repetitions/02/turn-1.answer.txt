Yes—cache before nightly makes sense. I’d keep those four stages, with clear gates:

1. **Extract aggregations:** preserve existing results.
2. **Add the cache:** measure request frequency, hits/misses, and computation cost. Count hits too, so caching doesn’t hide demand.
3. **Move selected heavy aggregations to nightly:** use those measurements and only move results that can tolerate overnight freshness.
4. **Delete the old path:** after verifying coverage and correctness through the replacement.

“Hot” and “expensive” are separate signals; both should inform stage three. I’ve saved the sequence in project memory.
