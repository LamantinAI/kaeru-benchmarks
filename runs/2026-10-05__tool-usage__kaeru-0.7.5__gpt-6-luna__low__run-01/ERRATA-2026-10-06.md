ignored `nodes.status`. The original D2 fixture declares an open hypothesis,
but its before/after exports contain no status tags. The intended open-claim
queue was therefore not faithfully materialized. Its queue-closure result must
be qualified rather than treated as a clean test of the intended initial state.

finding does not change the original 21 zero-call cases or rescue memory adoption.

not a newly source-validated aggregate. Attach this qualification when discussing
those counts. A separate full-policy run retained its similarly affected attempt
and executed one valid D2 after repairing the source-field mapping.
